What is the best luxury safari company in Kenya?

Horizon Legacy is a Kenyan owned luxury safari company based in Nairobi. One named designer plans your journey, we use only camps we have stayed in, and our own ground teams look after you on the day. Your designer will share verified guest feedback and credentials on request.

How much does a luxury safari in Kenya cost?

Kenya journeys start from USD 7,500 per person for a private road safari, from USD 11,000 per person for six days at Angama Mara, and from USD 16,450 per person for nine days across Amboseli, the Maasai Mara and Laikipia. Price depends on season, camps and how you fly between them.

How much does an ultra luxury safari cost?

From USD 15,150 per person for eight days across Angama Mara and Mara Plains Camp at low season. Our nine day Maasai Mara and Serengeti safari with Singita Sasakwa starts from USD 23,600, and the eight day Tanzania Grand Circuit across three private concessions from USD 30,100.

What is included in a luxury safari?

Every journey is private and includes your camps, a private guide and vehicle, the flights and road transfers we arrange within East Africa, and park fees. Most luxury camps include meals and game activities, some include drinks. International flights and visas are not included, and your designer confirms exactly what each camp includes before you book.

When is the best time to go on safari in East Africa?

All year. Calving in the southern Serengeti runs January to March, April and May bring green landscapes and the best value, gorilla season in Rwanda starts in June, and the Great Migration is in the Maasai Mara from July to October.

Where is the best place for gorilla trekking, Rwanda or Uganda?

Both are exceptional. Rwanda offers mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park, two hours from Kigali, with journeys from USD 21,950 per person. Uganda offers gorillas in Bwindi and chimpanzees in Kibale from USD 8,750 per person. June to September and December to February are best for both.

Can I combine gorilla trekking with Kenya and Tanzania?

Yes. Our 14 day Gorillas, the Crater, the Serengeti and the Maasai Mara journey links Rwanda, Tanzania and Kenya with a gorilla permit included, from USD 24,650 per person. The 12 day Ultimate East Africa Luxury Safari also links all three.

Do you offer small group luxury safaris?

Yes. The 12 day Ultimate East Africa Luxury Safari is a small group journey across Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania for 6 to 12 guests, from USD 46,200 per person, departing June 2027 and September 2027.

How far in advance should I book?

For gorilla trekking and the busiest Maasai Mara months, nine to twelve months ahead — the best camps and permits sell out first. For other months, three to six months is usually enough.

Can you combine a safari with the Seychelles or Mauritius?

Yes. We pair safaris in Kenya and Tanzania with private island stays in Zanzibar, the Seychelles and Mauritius, such as our nine day Kenya and Seychelles and ten day Kenya and Mauritius journeys.

Are your safaris suitable for families with children?

Yes. We plan family safaris with private vehicles, family suites and whole private villas, such as the Private Villa Family Safari in the Maasai Mara. Some camps set minimum ages and we plan around them.

Do you arrange flights and transfers?

We arrange every flight, private charter, road transfer and park fee within East Africa. International flights and visas are not included, and we guide you through both.
